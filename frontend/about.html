About PrivacyReady

Empowering UK businesses to achieve, maintain, and demonstrate UK GDPR compliance effortlessly without expensive legal consultancies or complex enterprise bloat.

Our Mission

PrivacyReady was founded to solve a fundamental problem facing UK small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs): traditional data protection consulting is prohibitively expensive, while generic consent widgets only address a fraction of legal requirements.

Our mission is to democratise data privacy compliance by providing an automated, end-to-end UK GDPR platform that continuously scans web assets, detects data protection risks, automates statutory documentation, and provides step-by-step remediation plans.

Why UK GDPR Matters

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and Data Protection Act 2018, organisations of all sizes face statutory obligations regarding transparency, consent management, subject access requests (DSRs), and Article 33 breach reporting. The Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) enforces fines up to £17.5 million or 4% of global annual turnover for non-compliance.

PrivacyReady gives UK business leaders total visibility over their compliance posture across 8 key pillars:

Automated Auditing

Deep scanning of web forms, cookies, third-party analytics trackers, and privacy policies.

Policy Generator

Automated creation of legally aligned privacy policies, cookie policies, and data processing agreements.

DSR & Breach Tracker

Streamlined management for 30-day Subject Access Requests and mandatory 72-hour ICO breach notifications.

Vendor ROPA

Records of Processing Activities and third-party vendor risk assessment tools under Article 30.

Our Principles

  • Plain English, Not Legalese: We translate complex UK data protection laws into clear, actionable advice.
  • Continuous Security: Data privacy is an ongoing practice, not a one-off audit. Our platform monitors your assets continuously.
  • Built for UK Business: Tailored specifically to the statutory frameworks enforced by the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
