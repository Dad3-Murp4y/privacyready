Cookies Policy

This policy explains how PrivacyReady Ltd ("we", "us") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website in compliance with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and UK GDPR.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, enhance user experience, and provide web analytics to site owners.

2. Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary to enable core site functionality, security, and network management. They do not store any personally identifiable information and cannot be disabled in our systems.

  • Session Security: Authenticates user sessions and prevents CSRF attacks.
  • Cookie Preferences: Remembers your consent preferences from our cookie banner.

3. Analytics Cookies (Consent Required)

We use aggregated performance analytics to understand visitor volume, traffic sources, and popular content. These cookies are set only after you explicitly grant consent via our consent banner. You may decline them at any time without restricting your access to our services.

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clearing your browser's cookies for privacyready.co.uk, which will re-trigger the consent banner on your next visit.
