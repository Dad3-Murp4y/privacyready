    Most UK websites are non-compliant — and don't know it yet

    The UK Data Protection Act 2018 and UK GDPR are strictly enforced by the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Regulators are actively auditing websites, and fines are being issued. Yet most businesses — especially SMEs — lack the legal and technical expertise to self-audit.

    PrivacyReady bridges that gap. Our automated audit scans your entire site — frontend, backend, cookies, forms, third-party scripts — and delivers a prioritized fix list with implementation guides.

    The 8 pillars of GDPR compliance

    The UK GDPR mirrors the EU's GDPR but adds local nuances. Our audit covers all 8 compliance pillars — from consent management to cross-border data transfers — ensuring you meet both the letter and spirit of the law.

    Each pillar is scored individually, so you know exactly where to focus first. No guesswork. No overwhelm. Just a clear compliance scorecard.
