Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about PrivacyReady and UK GDPR compliance automation.

What does the free scan check?

Our free scan checks your website for common UK GDPR compliance signals: HTTPS security headers, cookie consent banner functionality, privacy policy accessibility, third-party tracking pixels, data controller disclosures, and Data Subject Rights (DSR) options.

Is PrivacyReady a law firm?

No. PrivacyReady is an automated compliance software platform, not a legal practice. Our scanning reports and policy generation wizards provide automated compliance tooling and are not legal advice. For complex regulatory litigation or custom legal advice, we recommend consulting a registered data protection solicitor in the UK.

What happens to my scan results when I sign up?

When you run a free scan and then create an account, your scan results and audit history are automatically linked to your organisation dashboard so you never lose your compliance progress.

What is a Data Subject Request (DSR)?

A DSR occurs when an individual exercises their rights under UK GDPR (e.g. requesting access to their personal data or requesting account deletion). UK GDPR enforces a strict 30-day response window. PrivacyReady's DSR tracker logs, assigns, and manages these deadlines automatically.

How do I contact support?

You can reach our team anytime at support@privacyready.co.uk or via our contact page. We respond to all queries within 1 business day.
